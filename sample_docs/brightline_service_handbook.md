# Brightline Lighting — Customer Service Handbook

This handbook is a fictional sample document for testing the Document Chat application. Brightline Lighting is an invented company, and none of the products, prices or policies below are real.

## 1. About Brightline

Brightline Lighting designs and sells LED luminaires for offices, warehouses, streets and sports facilities. The company was founded in 2009 in Graz and today employs around 340 people across three sites: the headquarters and design studio in Graz, a production plant in Maribor, and a logistics hub in Linz. Brightline sells directly to installers and facility managers and through a network of 45 certified partners in Central Europe.

Our mission is to replace inefficient legacy lighting with durable, repairable fixtures. Every luminaire is designed so that the LED module and the driver can be replaced separately without tools, which extends the useful life of the housing to more than twenty years.

## 2. Product lines

Brightline organises its catalogue into four product lines.

The Aurora line covers recessed and surface-mounted office panels. The most popular model is the Aurora AP-600, a 600 by 600 millimetre panel with 34 watts of power and 4,400 lumens. It has a unified glare rating below 19, which makes it suitable for workplaces with computer screens.

The Halden line covers high-bay luminaires for warehouses and production halls. The Halden HB-150 delivers 21,000 lumens at 150 watts and is rated IP65, so it is protected against dust and water jets. It can be mounted at heights between six and fourteen metres.

The Vega line covers street and area lighting. The Vega SL-80 street light uses 80 watts, has a colour temperature of 3,000 kelvin to reduce light pollution, and includes a seven-pin NEMA socket for smart city controllers.

The Stadion line covers floodlights for sports fields. The Stadion FL-1000 floodlight has 1,000 watts, an asymmetric beam and flicker-free operation suitable for television broadcasting.

## 3. Warranty

All Brightline luminaires come with a standard warranty of five years from the date of delivery. The Vega and Stadion lines, which are used outdoors, carry an extended warranty of seven years. The warranty covers failures of the LED module, the driver and the housing that are caused by material or manufacturing defects.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by incorrect installation, voltage surges above the rated limits, vandalism, or the use of cleaning agents that contain solvents. Normal lumen depreciation is not a defect as long as the luminaire still produces at least 80 percent of its initial light output after 50,000 operating hours.

To make a warranty claim, the customer must submit the claim in writing through the service portal within 30 days of discovering the defect. The claim must include the serial number, the invoice number and a photo of the product label. Brightline will decide within ten working days whether the product is repaired, replaced or credited.

## 4. Ordering and pricing

Orders can be placed through the partner portal, by email to the sales team, or by phone. The minimum order value for direct orders is 250 euros. Orders below that value are handled by our certified partners.

List prices are published every January and remain valid for the full calendar year. Volume discounts start at 50 units, with 8 percent off the list price, and rise to 15 percent for orders of more than 500 units. Framework agreements for multi-year projects are negotiated individually with the key account team.

Payment terms are 30 days net from the invoice date. Customers who pay within 10 days receive a 2 percent early payment discount. For first-time customers, Brightline may require prepayment for orders above 20,000 euros.

## 5. Delivery and shipping

Standard products are shipped from the Linz logistics hub within three working days of order confirmation. Custom configurations, such as special colour temperatures or housing colours, have a lead time of four to six weeks.

Shipping is free of charge for orders above 1,500 euros within Austria, Germany and Slovenia. For smaller orders, a flat shipping fee of 45 euros applies. Deliveries to other EU countries are charged at cost.

Every shipment is sent with a tracking number by email. The customer must check the delivery for visible transport damage and note any damage on the delivery receipt. Hidden transport damage must be reported within five working days, otherwise the carrier will not accept the claim.

## 6. Returns

Unused products in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. A restocking fee of 15 percent of the net product value is charged to cover inspection and repackaging. Custom configurations and products that have been installed cannot be returned.

To start a return, the customer requests a return authorisation number, known as an RMA number, through the service portal. Parcels without an RMA number printed on the outside will be refused at the warehouse. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days after the returned goods have been inspected.

## 7. Installation guidance

All Brightline luminaires must be installed by a qualified electrician in accordance with local regulations. Before installation, the power supply must be switched off and secured against being switched back on.

Aurora panels can be installed in standard suspended ceilings using the included clips, or surface-mounted with the optional frame kit AK-600. Halden high-bays are supplied with a hook and a safety cable; the safety cable must always be attached to the building structure independently of the hook.

Dimmable versions support DALI-2 and 1-10 volt dimming. When DALI-2 is used, each luminaire must be addressed during commissioning with the free Brightline Commission app, which is available for Android and iOS.

## 8. Maintenance

LED luminaires need very little maintenance, but regular cleaning keeps the light output high. We recommend cleaning indoor luminaires once a year and outdoor luminaires every six months. Use a soft cloth with water and a mild, pH-neutral detergent. Never use high-pressure cleaners on Aurora panels.

The LED module and driver are designed as replaceable spare parts. Spare parts remain available for at least ten years after a product has been discontinued. Replacing a driver takes less than five minutes and does not require removing the luminaire from the ceiling.

## 9. Customer support

The technical support team is available Monday to Friday from 7:30 to 17:00 Central European Time. Customers can reach support by phone, by email, or through the chat function in the service portal. Urgent issues affecting public street lighting are handled by an on-call engineer, who can be reached around the clock through the emergency hotline.

The support team answers emails within one working day. For complex lighting design questions, customers can book a free 45-minute video consultation with one of our lighting designers. Lighting simulations in DIALux are offered free of charge for projects with more than 100 luminaires.

## 10. Sustainability

Brightline is committed to reaching net-zero emissions across its own operations by 2030. The Maribor production plant has been powered entirely by renewable electricity since 2021, and its roof carries a 1.2 megawatt solar installation.

All packaging is made from recycled cardboard and contains no plastic foam. At the end of a product's life, customers can send luminaires back through the Brightline take-back programme, which is free of charge for customers in the EU. More than 92 percent of the material in a returned luminaire is recycled.

## 11. Training and certification

Brightline runs a certification programme for installers. The two-day Certified Installer course covers product selection, installation, DALI-2 commissioning and troubleshooting. Participants who pass the final exam receive a certificate that is valid for three years.

Certified installers receive an additional year of warranty on every project they register in the partner portal, as well as priority access to technical support. The course costs 390 euros per participant and is held four times a year in Graz.
